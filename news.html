What's New at Fizzwhizz

Keep this page bookmarked — we post company news here as it happens, so you'll always be the first to know what Fizzwhizz Candy Co. is up to.

November 2, 1998 — We're on the Internet! After months of planning, Fizzwhizz Candy Co. is proud to announce the launch of this, our very first web site. Now candy lovers everywhere can learn about our products, enter our contests, and find a store near them, right from their home computer. We think this "World Wide Web" thing is going to be big, and we're excited to be a part of it.

October 12, 1998 — Rainbow Blast Gum hits stores nationwide. Our research and development team's newest creation, Rainbow Blast Gum, is now shipping to grocery and convenience stores across the country. Using our proprietary Color-Shift Technology, this gum actually changes color while you chew it. Early feedback from taste-testers has been outstanding — see our Products page for full details.

September 3, 1998 — Guess the flavor, win the game. Mystery Blast Twist-Pops are now available at participating retailers. Each pop is wrapped in plain silver foil, so nobody knows the flavor until the first lick — not even our own staff! We've already had dozens of letters from customers trying to guess the full flavor lineup.

June 18, 1998 — New store opens in Springfield. We're proud to announce the opening of our newest Fizzwhizz retail counter, located inside the Springfield Mall food court. Stop by for the full product line, plus in-store-only sampling of our latest flavors. See our About Us page for store hours and directions.

March 4, 1998 — Fizzwhizz featured at the Regional Candy & Snack Expo. Representatives from Fizzwhizz Candy Co. attended this year's Regional Candy & Snack Expo, where our SuperSour Screamers were named a "Show Favorite" by attendees. Thank you to everyone who stopped by our booth — we're already looking forward to next year.
