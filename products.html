Our Products

Every Fizzwhizz candy is developed by our own research and development staff, right here at company headquarters. Below you'll find our current line-up — read on to learn what makes each one special, and watch this page, because we're always working on the next new thing.

Power Pops

Our best-selling lollipop, now charged with our proprietary FIZZ-ACTION Crystals for a fizzing sensation you can actually feel on your tongue. Available in Cherry Blast, Grape Storm, and Tropical Punch. Look for the gold wrapper at your favorite store.

Rainbow Blast Gum

Using our all-new Color-Shift Technology, Rainbow Blast Gum actually changes color as you chew — starting out blue and turning bright pink right before your eyes! It's the most talked-about gum in the schoolyard, and we're proud to have invented it right here at Fizzwhizz.

SuperSour Screamers

For candy fans who like it extra sour, SuperSour Screamers are coated in our specially-formulated sour sugar crystal blend. We warn you now: these are sour enough to make you scream! Comes in a re-sealable pouch so you can share with friends — or keep them all to yourself.

Crackle Poppers

Crackle Poppers are little candy pieces that pop and crackle right in your mouth. Kids and grown-ups alike can't get enough of the crackling sound and the sweet fruit flavor underneath. A Fizzwhizz classic since 1988, and still one of our most requested treats.

Mystery Blast Twist-Pops

Can you guess the flavor? Each Mystery Blast Twist-Pop is individually wrapped in plain silver foil so the flavor stays a surprise until you take your first lick. We've had customers write in from all across the country trying to guess our secret flavor list — so far, nobody's gotten all of them right!
