About Fizzwhizz Candy Co.

Fizzwhizz Candy Co. was founded in 1974 by the Fizzwhizz family with a single goal: to bring a little more fizz, fun, and flavor into candy aisles everywhere. What started as one small storefront has grown into a company shipping candy to stores across the country — but we're still family-owned, and we still test every new flavor right here at our headquarters before it ever reaches a store shelf.

We're proud to have brought this website online, and we hope it makes it easier than ever for candy fans everywhere to keep up with what we're making, enter our promotions, and find a Fizzwhizz counter near them.
